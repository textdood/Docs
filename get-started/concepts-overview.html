Concepts overview

A map of the ideas you’ll see across Dood — so the rest of the docs (and the product) read the same language.

Goals

Outcomes you’re building toward — school, career, gym, a project, or everyday life. Goals are what hours should feed.

Blocks

Time on the calendar reserved for work. Proposals become blocks when you accept them into your schedule.

Planning

The AI loop that turns plain-language intent into schedule proposals you can accept, edit, or decline.

Coach

The messaging agent that checks in, nudges on missed habits, and keeps replies low-friction — not a second inbox to manage.

Streaks

Consecutive follow-through on habits or commitments. Streaks make consistency visible day to day.

XP

Experience points earned by showing up. XP gamifies progress without replacing the real signal: time spent on goals.

Life mode

Your intensity dial — how often Dood reaches out, how direct it is, and how much rest it plans around.

Integrations

Connected apps (Calendar, Strava, Discord, and more) that give Dood context from life outside the product.

Goals and time

Dood treats goals as the reason for the calendar. Week views and goal bars help answer a simple question: which goals got fed, and which starved? Follow-through isn’t a feeling — it’s hours you can see.

Proposals, then calendar

Planning doesn’t silently rewrite your life. Dood suggests time-blocked proposals first. You stay in control: accept what fits, decline what doesn’t, then sync with Google Calendar when connected.

Coach behavior

The coach should feel like someone who texts at the right time — not a calendar alarm. Good nudges are specific, short, and easy to answer (yes/no or pick 1 / 2 / 3). Quiet hours and daily limits keep intensity in check.

Progress signals

Streaks and XP reinforce consistency. Analytics, quests, and leaderboards (where available) add optional social and game layers on top of the core loop: plan → show up → see the result.

Surfaces

  • Web app — Dashboard, calendar, planning, goals, profile
  • Mobile — On-the-go access to the same accountability loop
  • Desktop companion — Local presence for screen-time and focus context
  • Messaging channels — Where proactive coach messages land

Keep going

If you’re brand new, start with What is Dood? and Quick start. Deeper topic pages for calendar, coach, streaks, and integrations will plug into this map as we write them.
