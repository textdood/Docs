Quick start

Get from signup to a first planned goal in a few minutes. You can refine settings later — this path is enough to feel the loop.

  1. Create your account Sign up for Dood and sign in. You’ll land in onboarding the first time you join.
  2. Tell Dood who you are Set your name, why you want a coach, and (optionally) how you found Dood. These answers personalize tone and recommendations — they aren’t permanent.
  3. Pick your life mode Choose how tough the coach should be. This shapes how often Dood reaches out, how direct messages feel, and how much rest gets planned in. You can change it anytime in profile settings.
  4. Set a first goal Describe what you’re trying to get done. Dood drafts a plan with concrete steps so you’re not starting from a blank calendar.
  5. Open your dashboard After onboarding, use Today, Calendar, and Planning to review proposals, accept blocks, and see the week you’re building.
  6. Connect real context (recommended) Link Google Calendar so Dood respects events you already have. Connect a messaging channel (like Discord) so the coach can check in where you’ll actually see it. Add Strava or other integrations when you’re ready.

Tip

You don’t need every integration on day one. A goal + calendar sync + one messaging channel is enough to make planning and nudges useful.

What “set up” looks like

  • At least one active goal with time on the calendar
  • Calendar sync so proposals don’t collide with real life
  • Coach channel connected if you want proactive check-ins
  • Life mode and reminder preferences that match your week

After that

Learn how profile settings work in Account and profile, or read the Concepts overview so terms like blocks, streaks, and life mode make sense everywhere else in the docs.
