What is Dood?

Dood (Day One or One Day) is an accountability system that turns intent into a real schedule — then helps you show up.

Most productivity tools help you decide what to do. Dood is built for follow-through: plan with AI, put work on a calendar you actually keep, and see which goals you’re feeding week to week.

How it works

Dood sits on three pillars:

  • AI calendar & planning — Describe what you want in plain language. Dood proposes time-blocked plans you accept or decline before anything hits your schedule.
  • Messaging coach — Check-ins and nudges land where you already live (Discord today; SMS and more over time), so accountability isn’t one more app to forget.
  • Streaks, XP & visibility — Progress is tracked and visualized so you can see short-term habits and long-term goals in one place — not as a vibe, as evidence.

Context from the apps you already use

Integrations pull signal from tools you already use day to day — for example Google Calendar and Strava — so Dood understands your real week, not just what you typed into a to-do list.

In short

Intent becomes a schedule. The coach keeps you honest. Integrations and streaks show whether you’re actually building the life you said you wanted.

Who it’s for

Dood is a strong fit if you:

  • Know what you want but struggle to protect time for it
  • Want a coach that texts at the right moment — not constant noise
  • Care about seeing which goals got hours this week and which starved
