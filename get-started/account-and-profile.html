Account and profile

Your profile is the control panel for identity, coach intensity, connected apps, and how Dood reaches you.

Open Profile from the dashboard to manage settings. Sections below match how the settings page is organized.

Account

Account covers the basics tied to your login: display name, username, and email. Sign out from here when you need to switch devices or accounts.

  • Display name — How Dood and the coach address you
  • Username — Your handle inside the product
  • Email — The address used for auth and account mail

Display

Choose how the web app looks. Theme options live here so you can keep Dood readable in light or dark environments without changing coach behavior.

Preferences

Preferences shape how ambitious and intense Dood feels day to day — especially life mode, which sets how often the coach reaches out, how blunt messages are, and how much rest gets planned into your week.

Life mode

You picked this during onboarding. Changing it later is normal — dial intensity up when you want pressure, or down when life is already loud.

Connections

Connections are the bridges between Dood and the rest of your stack:

  • Messaging — Discord, SMS, and other channels the coach can use
  • Calendar & fitness — Google Calendar, Strava, and similar integrations that feed real-world context
  • AI models — Options for web planning and bot behavior where available on your plan

Notifications

Reminder and nudge preferences control when Dood is allowed to interrupt you. Use these to set quiet expectations — caps, channels, and reminder style — so accountability stays helpful instead of noisy.

Billing and plan

Plan and billing settings show what you’re on and let you manage subscription details (including checkout and customer portal flows when applicable). Pricing pages in the app describe what’s included on each tier.

Danger zone

Destructive account actions (like deleting data) live behind an explicit confirmation flow. Treat these as permanent — export or disconnect integrations first if you need a soft exit.
